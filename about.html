01 / WHO WE ARE

A Nigerian hotel in Edo

Built around the idea that you should feel welcome.

Happy's Villa is a modern Nigerian hotel in Benin City, Edo State — relaxed enough for a weekend, polished enough for business, and warm enough for family visits.

We take inspiration from the energy of Benin City, Edo heritage and the generous spirit of Nigerian hospitality. From the food we serve to the warmth of our welcome, the Nigerian identity of Happy's Villa is part of the experience. Our spaces draw from the warmth of Nigerian homes and the character of Edo. We want the Villa to feel comfortable rather than cold, colourful without being loud, and practical for the way our guests actually travel.

Whether you're visiting family, attending an event, travelling for work or simply taking a break, our team is here to make the stay straightforward and memorable.
